An open agentic website

Published for any agent

The network is a data-only Neuro SAN JSON file listed in the site manifest. Another site loads it by this address and runs it in its own visitor's browser:

loose_change_agent

It shares two tools with callers, both in the visitor's browser and keeping nothing: categorise_merchants (statement wording in, categories out) and review_statement_privately (opens a window here, where only the visitor picks the file, then sends back a summary they have read).

Your key, your choice

This site pays for no AI calls. In the settings under the chat pick Google AI Studio, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Otari or a local model. The key stays in this browser and goes only to the provider you pick. Everything is open: source on GitHub.

Loose Change does not give financial advice. It adds up what your statement already says and shows you where to look. Lead developer: Khozhijon, who led the project and directed an AI pair-programmer (Claude Code) that helped write the code.
