PocketbookDemo: another agentic site that calls Loose Change

Your monthly budget

Pocketbook is a tiny budgeting page. Its assistant has no way to read a bank statement, so it asks the Loose Change agent, a different website. That site's network runs here, in your browser, on your own AI key.

Ask Pocketbook

Try “Review my bank statement privately and tell me which budget lines I am over.” You will get a card asking whether Pocketbook may contact Loose Change, then a window on that site where you pick the file.
